What this is

A collection, not a job board. Companies do not register here and nothing is posted — every listing is pulled automatically from company career pages and public job APIs, then filtered down to design roles only. It refreshes itself every day.

Where the listings come from

Verified and reported listings

There is no gatekeeper here. Anyone with a free GitHub account can vouch for a listing or flag it — the buttons on every card open a pre-filled form. Counts are public and auditable in the repo's issue tracker, and the site rebuilds with them every morning.

Worth reporting: any "internship" that asks you to pay a registration, training or security fee. That is the most common scam in Indian design hiring, and it is never legitimate.

Missing something?

Many Indian studios hire only over email and never post publicly — those are in the Companies tab instead. If a company is missing, open an issue or a pull request on the repo. Instagram-only postings cannot be collected automatically — Instagram blocks it — so those go in by hand.
